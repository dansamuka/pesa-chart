A Kenyan re-scaling of xkcd #980 · sourced, August 2026

The Pesa Chart

Randall Munroe's "Money" chart put a boda-boda fare and the U.S. federal budget on the same page, one square at a time. This is the same idea, rebuilt from scratch in shillings — five tiers, each one thousand times bigger than the last, from a plate of ugali to the national debt.

Reference: xkcd.com/980 — Randall Munroe, 2011. Every figure below is Kenyan and current to 2025/26; none of the original's numbers are reused.
KES 1 — Everyday prices KES 1,000 — Personal finances KES 1,000,000 — Mid-scale wealth KES 1 billion — Companies & counties KES 1 trillion — The nation
10⁰ – 10³

Everyday prices

Each square is one shilling. This is the tier of transport, groceries, and small change — what most transactions in Kenya actually look like.

10³ – 10⁶

Personal finances

Each square is one thousand shillings (KES 1,000). Rent, household spending and salaries — with all salary figures shown on a monthly basis for direct comparison.

10⁶ – 10⁹

Mid-scale wealth

Each square is one million shillings (KES 1M). Cars and other million-shilling benchmarks, alongside what an average Kenyan versus an average Nairobian produces in a year.

10⁹ – 10¹²

Companies & counties

Each square is one billion shillings (KES 1B). Where personal wealth stops and institutional money starts: listed companies, county governments, and Kenya's wealthiest individuals.

10¹² +

The nation

Each square is one trillion shillings (KES 1T). The budget, the debt, and the entire economy those first four tiers add up into.

Sources & method

  • Each tier's unit is 1,000× the tier before it — the same nested-scale structure as the original xkcd chart, re-based for the shilling.
  • Square counts are the real figure divided by the tier's unit, rounded to one decimal place. Nothing is scaled for visual effect.
  • National budget, revenue, and county-transfer figures: National Treasury Budget Policy Statement / Budget Statement FY2025/26; KPMG and Grant Thornton budget bulletins (June 2025).
  • GDP: Kenya National Bureau of Statistics, Economic Survey 2026 (KES 17.58 trillion, 2025 nominal).
  • Public debt: National Treasury Annual Public Debt Management Report 2024/25; Kenyan Wallstreet debt coverage (Jan 2026 bulletin).
  • Corporate results: Safaricom PLC FY2026 audited results, EABL FY2026 full-year results, and KCB Group Plc FY2025 audited results.
  • Wages: Regulation of Wages (General) (Amendment) Order 2024; KNBS-sourced average/median earnings (2024/25). Salary entries are normalized to monthly figures.
  • Everyday prices: KNBS Consumer Price Index basket; EPRA fuel pricing; retail survey reporting, July 2025.
  • Net worth: Henley & Partners / New World Wealth Africa Wealth Report 2025; independent Kenyan business-press estimates — flagged as modelled estimates, not audited figures.
  • Nairobi County budget: Nairobi City County Fiscal Strategy Paper and FY2025/26 budget estimates.

USD figures converted at approximately KES 129 per USD (mid-2025 average rate). Some figures are financial-year totals (ending March or June) rather than calendar-year; each label notes the period where it matters. Net-worth and informal-market prices are estimates and vary by source — treat them as orientation, not accounting.
